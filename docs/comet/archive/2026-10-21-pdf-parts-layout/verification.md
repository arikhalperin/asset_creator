## Risks and skipped work

- images::load_page accepts non-finite or huge overrides: inf or 1e9 dpi gives a 1x1 px page. This is unreachable from the UI, because the DragValue clamps to 50-4800, but the API does not guard against it.
- A new export with fewer sheets, or without PNGs, leaves stale <stem>_NN.png files from an earlier export next to the new PDF.
- If a loader or export worker thread panics, the tab stays in 'loading' or 'exporting' because a disconnected channel is not detected. I could not trigger this with corrupt inputs: decode errors are skipped.
- Whole images are decoded and resampled in memory, so a page near A0 at 300 dpi needs about 0.5 GB.
- A one-off UI frame of about 150 ms can occur when a large multi-page load finishes (final rebuild). It was 38-50 ms in other runs; p95 stays under 0.4 ms.
